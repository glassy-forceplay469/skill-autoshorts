## 📋 What this tool does

Skill-autoshorts automates the process of finding popular moments in long videos. It uses intelligent software to watch your chosen long-form videos. It transcribes the audio, identifies interesting segments, and highlights the best moments. You receive a notification on your phone asking for your final choice. Once you approve a clip, the system prepares it for social media platforms. Over time, the software reviews your past engagement data to learn what viewers prefer. This loop helps you pick better moments as the system grows smarter.

## 📈 Understanding the learning process

The software uses engagement data to refine its selection process. It monitors metrics such as views, likes, and watch time. You do not need to perform manual data entry. The system syncs this information automatically from your social media accounts. 

When you notice the system suggesting clips that perform better, it means the learning cycle reached a mature stage. You can reset these data patterns at any time in the settings menu if you change your content strategy.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting common issues

If the software fails to process a video, check your internet first. Large files require a consistent connection to the cloud servers.

If you stop receiving notifications:
1. Open the app settings menu.
2. Check your phone number or notification token.
3. Click the test notification button.

If the app feels slow while processing:
1. Close other heavy programs like video editors or games.
2. Ensure at least 20GB of space remains on your drive.
3. Restart the application to clear the temporary cache.

If you encounter errors during the upload process:
1. Check your social media login status in the settings tab.
2. Re-authenticate your account if the app alerts you.
3. Verify that your social profile allows third-party tools to post.
